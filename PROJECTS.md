## RouteX

RANNTA's native routing, execution-planning, blockchain-normalization and registry architecture.

RouteX is designed as the independent RANNTA routing layer for exchange, wallet, cross-chain and developer surfaces. Production routing implementation remains private while public architecture and identity boundaries are documented through the RANNTA X-Change repository.
